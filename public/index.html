STEP-BY-STEP ORCHESTRATION

Seven steps from repo to verified merge.

Automated deterministic verification that executes in seconds directly on your machine without custom YAML files.

01
Initialize & Connect
Run anvil init in one command. ANVIL prepares local baseline storage in .anvil/ with zero telemetry.
02
Zero-Config Stack Detection
Automatically scans package.json, tsconfig, requirements.txt, or Cargo.toml to resolve languages and test runners.
03
AST Architecture Mapping
Maps functions, classes, interfaces, circular dependencies, and entrypoints in <1.4k compact tokens.
04
Secret & Sandbox Audit

Blocks unapproved structural drift, secret leaks, and regressions. Emits standard SARIF reports for pull requests.
